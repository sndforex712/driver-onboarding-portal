---
name: Case Detail Progress Rail
description: Responsive Recruiting case-detail progress visualization and its workflow-preservation boundary.
---

The case-detail status rail is a presentation layer only. It uses the shared canonical Recruiting stage utility for desktop/tablet milestones and a compact mobile status header. The separate workflow action card and timeline remain visible and functional.

**Why:** The progress visualization must become legible across viewports without removing task ownership, next-action, due-time, transfer, or timeline context that operators depend on.

**How to apply:** Keep stage ordering and status semantics centralized. When refining the rail, preserve the independent workflow and timeline panels; do not treat their task/deadline content as part of the rail.
